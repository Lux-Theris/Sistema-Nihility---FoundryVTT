# Inventário, munição, consumíveis e carregador

> Parte do guia do projeto. O índice e as regras que valem em todo lugar estão no `CLAUDE.md` da raiz. Quando o texto diz "abaixo"/"acima" ou cita uma seção que não está aqui, procure-a pelo índice.

## Inventário, Porão e Munição — `module/economy/inventory.js` + pure rules in `config.js`

Behind `FEATURES.inventory` (default on; options `carryPerStrength`/`carryPerDefense`) and its sub-block `encumbrance` (default off). Pure and tested: `stackCount`, `inventoryLoad` (a container always takes 1 slot of its holder; what is inside uses the container's slots and weighs `× (1 − weightReduction)`; an unknown `containerId` counts as loose), `currencyWeight`, `carryCapacity`, `encumbrancePenalty` (excess % → −% movement, capped at 100), `speciesCarry` (preset `inventorySlots`/`carryBase`, else `SPECIES_CARRY_DEFAULTS`, else 10/30), `cargoSlotsFor`, `cargoMassFactor`, `ammoFitsLauncher`. `CharacterDataModel` derives `system.inventory` in `deriveInventory` (after combat attributes, **before** `deriveMovement`, which subtracts `inventory.penalty` from the movement %); item containers are Item Geral `container.enabled`, skill containers `grantsContainer.enabled`; items point at them through `containerId`. Overflow only **warns**. `handleItemDrop` (both sheets override `_onDropItem` with it) stacks identical Item Geral (same name + img + place, no containers), moves between Actors the user owns (copy from Directory/Compendium), and puts an item in a container when dropped on `[data-container-id]`. Ships: cargo = their Item Geral, slots from `cargo`-role modules (`cargoSlots`), and with `encumbrance` on `engineRatioFor` is divided by `cargoMassFactor` (Porte `massReference`). Ammo: `ammoTypesData` catalog (`getAmmoTypes`, flat list editor `AmmoTypesConfigApp`), weapon module `usesAmmo`/`ammoTypes` (toggled via `toggleAmmoType` chips), Item Geral `ammo` block; `fireStarshipWeapon` → `pickLauncherAmmo` uses the ammo's formula/elements/absolute, adds its `penetrationBonus`, and spends 1.

## Consumíveis, cargas e carregador de arma — `module/economy/consumables.js` + `consumable-rules.js` (1.67.0)

Pure and tested in `consumable-rules.js`: `consumeUse` (no charges → 1 from the stack; with `charges` → 1 charge of the open unit, `chargesUsed` counts, at the end 1 leaves the stack and the next starts full; the last unit deletes the Item), `chargesLeft`, `magazineState`, `ammoFitsWeapon`, `resolveEffectAmount`.

- **Consumível** (`GenericItemDataModel.consumable`, tab "Consumível" on the Item sheet, led): Efeito or Dano, Em si / Num alvo / Área. `consumableMech(item)` builds a Skill-shaped mechanic (level 1, cost 0, `sourceMagic` only with Dano Mágico — a potion isn't dispelled by Antimagia) and `useConsumable` runs it through the **same** paths as a Skill (`rollSkillDamage`/`rollSkillDamageArea` with Escala por Atributo — that is what keeps a grenade relevant against the quadratic Vida curve — and `applySkillEffects`/`applySkillEffectsArea`, now exported). No XP (not a Skill). Cancelling the target/area spends nothing (`pickAreaTargets` without `origin` = cancelled); a placed grenade with nobody inside is spent. "Usar" sits on inventory rows (no need to be equipped) and on the Ficha gear list; consumables can go on the hotbar (`hotbarKind` "consumable" calls `useConsumable` directly).
- **The effect editor is shared**: the Skill's effect cards are the partial `nihility.effectCards` (`templates/parts/effect-cards.hbs`, hash `effects`, `hasUpkeep`); `NihilityItemSheet#effectsPath()` is `system.effects` on a Skill and `system.consumable.effects` on an Item Geral — clone/write/element-chip paths all go through it.
- **"% do máximo"** (`amountMode: "flat"|"percentMax"` on every effect entry): for Vida, Mana, Escudo and the instant targets, resolved per target at application (`resolveEffectAmount`, `PERCENT_TARGETS` in effects-apply.js; Escudo uses Vida máxima). A "pts / % máx." select sits next to the amount in the card.
- **Instant heal targets** `heal` ("Curar Vida (na hora)") and `restoreEnergy`: the old `hp`/`energy` targets **without** Periódico are a temporary buff to the *maximum*, never a heal — healing only existed as a periodic tick. `restoreVital` heals now through the same plan as the tick (heal blocks by level, 0% parts cap, body-part split; negative = direct loss, no defences). New consumable effects default to "Curar Vida 25% do máximo".
- **Carregador** (`weapon.magazineSize` 0 = infinite as before, `loaded`, `ammoTypes`, `loadedAmmo`): each attack spends 1 (`useWeaponAttack`), empty refuses before asking for a target (`weaponCanFire`). "Recarregar" (`reloadWeapon`, ⟳ always visible: it is also *trocar munição*) spends 1 Item Geral with the `ammo` block of an accepted type (the same ammo Items ships use; none checked = any) and snapshots it in `loadedAmmo` — its formula, elements and Dano Absoluto replace the weapon's on each shot; `loadedAmmo.source` keeps the Item's data so it can come back. **What was inside returns to the inventory** (`reloadPlan`, pure/tested): full → back onto the normal stack; partial → an *open* unit with `ammo.rounds` = what was left (gives only those shots when loaded again, and `findStack` never stacks it with full ones); empty → nothing. Empty magazine with a single compatible ammo reloads directly; otherwise a dialog shows what is inside, the choices and "Só descarregar". **Carga própria da munição** (`ammo.capacity`, 0 = the weapon's `magazineSize`): a full unit fills up to *its* Carga (Célula grande 20, pequena 10), snapshotted as `loadedAmmo.capacity`; every reader goes through `magazineState({size, loaded, capacity})` (max = capacity or size; `uses` still comes from the weapon's `magazineSize`) and `reloadPlan({size, loaded, currentMax, ammoRounds, capacity})` judges "full" against the ammo that was inside. Overheat (rest of backlog C7) was left out on purpose.
