# Dano por elemento, condicionais, progressão e rolagem com modificadores

> Parte do guia do projeto. O índice e as regras que valem em todo lugar estão no `CLAUDE.md` da raiz. Quando o texto diz "abaixo"/"acima" ou cita uma seção que não está aqui, procure-a pelo índice.

## Dano por elemento, Imunidade, Escala e Dano Absoluto — `module/combat/damage-rules.js`

All pure and tested; `combat/damage-roll.js` only gathers the numbers.

- **Element hierarchy ("Subtipo de", `parent`, 1.62.0):** an element can be a subtype of another (factory: Físico › Cortante/Perfurante/Contundente, `PHYSICAL_SUBTYPES` in config.js, appended once to saved catalogs by the `physicalSubtypes` migration). Pure and tested in damage-rules.js: `elementAncestors` (cycle-safe), `chainedResistance` (own + each ancestor's Resistance multiply what passes — Geral → Físico → Cortante; ≥100% anywhere = immune), `inheritedElementEffects` (own on-hit effects first, then ancestors' with a different type/target key), and `buildAffinityMatrix` inherits the parent's row and, against a subtype, the parent's column. `applyDamageReductions`' `resistanceFor` builds the chain through `damageElementChain(id)` (config.js) and credits Resistance XP to the own element's Skill, else the nearest ancestor's; `elementContext` reads the inherited effects. `damageElementPathLabel` ("Físico › Cortante") labels the Resistance target dropdown. Validar Mundo flags an unknown/self parent and cycles.
- **Split by element** (`splitDamageParts`/`resolveDamageParts`): a hit is divided into **equal** parts, one per element. Each part goes through attacker bonus → Defesa Mágica (only if magical) → Resistência Geral → the Resistência of **its own** element. Immunity (Resistance ≥ 100%) zeroes only that part.
- **Element on-hit effects** (`effects[]` on each entry of the Tipos de Dano catalog, card editor `apps/damage-elements-config.js`, types in `NIHILITY.ELEMENT_EFFECT_TYPES`):
  - `condition` with chance: uses the Condition's default effect with that part's final damage;
  - `traitBonus`: +% if the target has a Trait;
  - `shieldDrain`: extra damage that only exists against a shield — the ship's Escudo layer, or the personal Escudo through `shieldExtra` in the damage card;
  - `penetration`: softens every defence proportionally but **never** crosses Immunity.
  Someone immune to the element is not affected by its effects. Elements also have a `group` (Físico/Fantasia/Energia/Exótico…), used to group the picker window; a saved catalog from before groups existed takes the group of the default element with the same id (`getActiveDamageElements`), so only GM-made elements land in "Outros". **Choosing elements (and Traços) never renders the whole catalog inline**: the form shows only the chosen ones as chips with × plus a "+ Elemento · N" button that opens `apps/checklist-picker.js` (filter + grouped checkboxes; ids no longer in the catalog stay listed under "Fora do catálogo" so Aplicar can't drop them silently). On document sheets it is the `nihility.elementChips` partial (`field` from `selectedElementChips`, actions `pickElements`/`removeElement`, where a `system.effects.N.damageElements` path is rewritten through the effects array); in plain-DOM forms (Skill editor modal, Species editor) it is `pickerFieldHtml`/`wirePickerField`/`readPickerField` with the list in `data-selected`. The inline chip grid is what grew into a wall once the Sci-Fi elements existed. Ship weapon Módulos now have `damageElements` too; ships have no per-element resistance, so the cascade uses the average of the parts' penetration/bonus/drain.
- **When conditions land:** for Personagem they are stored in the damage card flags and applied only when the GM clicks Aplicar (the hit is confirmed then; Desfazer removes the effects that click created). In an area roll they are only listed per target. For ships the cascade writes itself.
- **Vantagem entre elementos** (Pokémon-style; replaced the old structure-only `vsElement` effect, which `buildAffinityMatrix` still converts into levels when reading): each catalog element has `affinity: {defenderId: level}` with levels −2 Imune, −1 Ineficaz, 0 Neutro, 1 Efetivo, 2 Super efetivo; multipliers are world settings (`affinityImmune`/`affinityIneffective`/`affinityEffective`/`affinitySuperEffective`, `getAffinityConfig`). Pure and tested: `affinityMultiplier`, `elementVsDefender` (defender elements **multiply**), `hitAffinityFactor` (average over the hit's equal parts), `cycleAffinityLevel`, `buildAffinityMatrix`. What a defender *is*: `actorElements(actor)` (config.js) = Species `elements` + Active Effects flagged `bodyElement` (Skill effect target `bodyElement`, reuses the entry's `elementId`; no Active Effect change, only the flag) + Conditions with `elements`. Where it applies: per part in `resolveDamageParts` (`part.affinity`; it also returns `shieldBase`, the damage **without** the body affinity), personal shield pools (`pool.elements` from the shield Effect entry's `damageElements`; the damage card stores `shieldBase`/`bodyFactor`/`elementIds` and `consumeShieldPools` takes a `layerMultiplier(pool)`; what leaks to HP is multiplied back by `bodyFactor`), the ship cascade (shield layer × affinity vs `system.shieldElements` of online shield modules; casco/hull × affinity vs `actorElements(ship)`), and structures. Dano Absoluto ignores it. Editor: `DamageElementsConfigApp` subclasses the card-list factory app and adds a "Tabela de vantagens" view (left click up, right click down) built from the cards currently on screen, writing each card's hidden `affinity` field.
- **Dano Absoluto** (`isAbsoluteDamage` on Skill, Sub-Skill, personal weapon and ship weapon): **the whole damage, whatever elements come with it** — cannot be *resisted* (ignores Defesa Mágica, Resistências, Imunidade and the personal Escudo) and elements do not change it either (no Trait bonus, no penetration math; the parts only still record which element each piece is, so on-hit Conditions can trigger) — but can be *dodged*: against a ship the Evasão still applies and what hits goes straight to Integridade Estrutural. A Estrutura in the way still stops it (cover, not resistance).
- **Escala** (`FEATURES.scale`, off by default; catalog and Porte → scale map in `apps/scales-config.js`, factor in the feature option): damage is multiplied by `factor^(attack step − target step)` (`scaleMultiplier`), after roll modifiers and before defences. The attack's scale is the Skill/weapon `damageScale` field, or the attacker's. Personagem uses `system.scale` (empty = first step), ships follow their Porte.
- **Traços** (`traitsData` catalog, `apps/traits-config.js`): species presets carry `traits[]`, a sheet adds `system.traits` / removes `system.traitsRemoved`; `resolveActorTraits` (pure) / `actorTraits(actor)` give the effective list. Edited on both sheets through the `nihility.traits` partial and `helpers/traits-ui.js`, GM-only: "+ Traço" opens the picker with the effective Traços checked, and `pickActorTraits` writes the difference against the Species (not given by the Species → `traits`; given and unchecked → `traitsRemoved`). The Species editor uses the same picker field.

## Modificadores Condicionais ("Quando → Então") — `conditional-modifiers.js` + `conditional-context.js`

`conditionalModifiers[]` on Título, Skill and Item Geral (`conditionalModifiersSchema()` in `item-models.js`; edited through the `nihility.conditionalModifiers` partial). Chosen from menus, never typed as an expression — the PF2e predicate model, not Midi-QOL's free code (fragile, and the research showed it misfiring). `conditional-modifiers.js` is pure and tested (`matchesWhen`, `sumConditionalModifiers`); `conditional-context.js` collects the active sources (Título always; Item only equipped; Skill always, or only while on if it is an Active Skill) and builds the context (own HP%, own and opponent's Conditions, opponent's Traits, opponent is a ship, the hit's elements, "in combat"). The "Then" decides *when* a rule counts: `attributeFlat` is **continuous** — `deriveConditionalAttributes` adds it to `effectiveTotal`/`bonus` after `deriveVitalStats` (never to `total`, so never to Vida/Mana máxima, which is what prevents the Força ↔ Vida loop) and only accepts self conditions; `damagePercent` (multiplies damage per target, after Escala), `rollFlat` (added to attribute rolls, against the Token the user has targeted) and `resistancePercent` (the **defender's** rules, with the attacker as opponent, added inside `applyDamageReductions`) count at the moment of the action. "Opponent" is always the other side of the action. `registerConditionalRefresh` re-prepares combatants when a combat starts/ends, because "estou em combate" is read during data preparation.

## Progressão: XP, níveis de Skill e escala de dano

Three separate curves, all in `config.js`, all reachable without Foundry and all covered by the suite in `test/` — **add a test there for anything you change here.** The thing they have in common is the reason they exist: this system's power curve is **quadratic** (HP = `Atributo × Atributo × mult`, over points that grow linearly per level), so any rule that scales linearly gets swallowed by it within a few levels.

**XP** (`getXpForNextLevel(level)`): both Actors (`system.attributes.xp`) and Skills (`SkillDataModel.xp`) accumulate it, with the same treatment on both sides. The cap is derived and the value is clamped **during preparation** (`deriveExperience` in `character-model.js`, the equivalent block in `item-models.js`), so XP never banks a surplus across a level — `xpMax`/`xpReady`/`xpPercent` are all derived, never stored. The curve itself is the `xpFormula` setting (default `100 * @nivel`), evaluated through `Roll.safeEval` (arithmetic only, never user code) and falling back to the default on an invalid formula rather than breaking the sheet. It is **linear on purpose**: against a quadratic power curve, a linear XP cost keeps the price per point of power roughly flat, while a quadratic one turns into a disguised level ceiling. Filling the bar announces via Voz do Mundo (`kind: "xp-ready"`) and **never levels anything up** — Level Up stays the GM's click, same as everywhere else in this system.

**Níveis de Skill** (`skillLevelBonuses(level)` → `{power, cost}`, applied by `effectiveSkillCost(baseCost, level)`): a Skill's level advances on a fixed, repeating cycle — N levels of Poder (each multiplies the Skill's effect) then M of Desconto (each cuts its Custo), all four numbers being settings (`skillPowerPerLevel`/`skillDiscountPerLevel`/`skillCyclePower`/`skillCycleDiscount`, plus the `skillCostFloorPercent` floor). The cycle is fixed rather than a per-level table so a player always knows what the next level gives without consulting anything. Two deliberate rules that look like bugs if you don't know them: both bonuses are **multiplicative, never subtractive** (a subtractive −20% would hit the floor in 5 levels and make the whole rest of the curve inert; multiplicative reaches it around level 24 on defaults), and a **Desconto level that lands when the cost is already at the floor is converted into a Poder level** (without that, every Skill past the floor accumulates levels that deliver literally nothing — 13 dead levels by level 50 on defaults, each one having cost the GM a Level Up click). Resistance skills do **not** pass through here at all: they have their own 10%/level progression and their own cap (`computeResistancePercent`/`resistanceMaxLevel`).

**Escala de dano** (`damageScalingMultiplier(actor, attributeKey)`): a Skill can name a `scalingAttribute`, and its damage is then multiplied by `(Atributo.total)² ÷ damageScalingDivisor` (setting, default 10). The **quadratic shape is the point, not a flourish** — it's the only shape that tracks the HP curve, which is itself a product of two attributes. Any linear scaling (add the bonus, multiply by the total) gets eaten alive: a same-level target would go from ~24 hits at level 1 to ~200 at level 50. With the quadratic form the number of hits stays roughly constant across the whole campaign, and pacing is controlled by the divisor and the Skill's own dice formula. No `scalingAttribute` (`""`, the default) returns 1, so every Skill written before this rule deals exactly the damage it always did.

**XP de Resistência** (`resistanceXpGain(blocked, maxHp)`, credited from `combat/resistance.js`): a Resistance Skill earns XP by actually blocking damage — but the gain is proportional to the **fraction of the defender's own Vida that was saved**, never to the absolute number blocked. Same reason again: with damage scaling quadratically, absolute-damage XP would inflate on its own and the same defensive act would be worth dozens of times more XP late in a campaign than early. In fractions, blocking an equally dangerous hit is worth the same XP at any level, and a scratch keeps being worth 0. `resistanceLearnThreshold` (default 25, `0` disables) separately counts how many times an Actor has been hit by a given damage type before the system *suggests* the matching Resistance to the GM — a suggestion, never an automatic grant.

**XP por uso de Skill** (`FEATURES.skillUseXp`, on by default; options `skillXpFactor` = XP of "an effect the size of one whole Vida", default 100, and `skillXpFlatPercent`, default 10). Pure rule in `skills/skill-xp-rules.js` (`skillUseXp`, `effectEntryMeasures`, tested); `skills/skill-xp.js` reads sheets and writes (`grantSkillUseXp(skill|uuid, measures)` never throws, skips Item-granted Skills, goes through the `skillUseXp` relay action when the caller doesn't own the Skill — the GM recomputes with its own config and caps one request at one unit). Same anti-inflation reasoning as Resistance XP: everything is a **fraction**, each target at most 1. Measures, decided with the owner:
- damage: final damage ÷ the target's `vitalBase` (Vida máxima; ship = Escudo + Casco + Integridade max), credited **at roll time** (`damageXpMeasures`; area sums targets via `rollSkillDamageArea`'s `hits`);
- heal: amount ÷ Vida máxima; periodic heal/damage credits **each tick** to `effect.origin` (the Skill uuid) in `tickPeriodicEffect`;
- **Escudo and Barreira (owner's decision 08/11):** cast once → only the flat % when it appears; **maintained** (Escudo from an Active Skill; Estrutura with `untilDeactivated` or `manaBarrier`) → nothing on activation, then `skillXpBlockPercent`% (default 50) of (damage held ÷ protected target's Vida) each time it holds a hit. Personal Escudo credits at the damage card's Aplicar (`creditShieldBlocks` in damage-apply.js, from `shieldBlockCredits`, pure — each pool knows its Skill); Estrutura in `hitStructure`. Measure flag `block: true`. Regenerating a sustained Escudo earns nothing; Desfazer doesn't take the XP back;
- buff/debuff: amount ÷ the target's current value (attribute `total`, Vida/Mana max, or 100 for % targets), **capped per target** at the `skillXpBuffCapPercent` option (default 25%, owner's decision 08/11: measured against the current value, a +3 on someone with 3 or 0 was a whole unit and filled level 1 in one use; the measure carries `buff: true`, Escudo does not) — `applyEffectsToActor` hangs the measures on `summary.xp`. Active and one-shot Skills earn the same per use, and re-activating is not blocked (the GM adjudicates at the table);
- Condition, Structure raised, Zone created: the flat %; a Zone also earns what it does each turn (`tickZonesForCombatant`), and a Estrutura with contact damage what the contact deals (`applyStructureContactAsGm`);
- **Antimagia earns by what it disables**: `applyAntimagic` returns `summary.xp` (flat per effect cut, per Active Skill switched off and for the Suppression; Escudo cut ÷ Vida); an antimagic **field** Structure or a **Seal** Condition earns flat per target whose magic attack it nulled (`antimagicBetween` returns the responsible Skills; `chargeAntimagic` credits) and per magic Structure it dissolved (`enforceAntimagicOnStructures`). A surcharge that was paid disables nothing and earns nothing.
Weapons give no XP (not a Skill). XP still stops at the level cap; Level Up stays the GM's click.

## Rolagem com modificadores (shift+clique) — `module/core/roll-modifiers.js`

Every roll button in the sheets — attribute 🎲, Iniciativa, damage Skill, "Atacar" with a weapon, firing a ship weapon, ship damage Skill — opens `promptRollOptions` (`apps/roll-options-dialog.js`) when **Shift** is held, through `rollOptionsFromEvent(event, title)`, *before* the target picker; a normal click rolls exactly as before. The dialog offers Vantagem/Normal/Desvantagem (the **whole** d20 pool rolled twice, keep highest/lowest: `{F, F}kh`) and free rows of add/subtract/multiply/divide with any value and an optional reason. There is deliberately **no** critical-hit rule and no GM "next roll" button: the system can't know what an "attack roll" is, so whoever rolls writes what the scene asked for. The pure rules (`normalizeRollOptions`, `buildModifiedFormula`, `applyRollModifiers`, `describeRollOptions`) are tested. Two application points, on purpose: attribute and initiative rolls put the operations **in the formula** so the chat card shows the final result; damage puts Vantagem in the formula but applies the operations **to the number** after attribute scaling, Skill level and ship weapon bonuses, and before the target's reductions — because scaling happens outside the formula. Operations apply **in the order they were written** ("+5" then "×2" = (roll + 5) × 2), divisions floor, damage never goes below 0.

- **Fraqueza e Condições na origem (1.117.0):** a Resistência final de um elemento pode ser **negativa** (fraqueza da origem, sem piso: −100% dobra, −300% quadruplica). Ordem na parte do golpe: Defesa Mágica → Resistência Geral → Resistência do elemento (as duas reduzem, Penetração suaviza) → fraqueza geral → fraqueza do elemento (multiplicam por 1 + fraqueza, sem Penetração). Imunidade (resistência ≥ 100%) vale antes e a fraqueza não a fura; Dano Absoluto ignora tudo. A fraqueza nunca é "bloqueio": não gera XP de Resistência. Detalhes e o resto em [especies.md](especies.md).
