# Espécies, Linhagens, Heranças, Evolução e Anatomia

> Parte do guia do projeto. O índice e as regras que valem em todo lugar estão no `CLAUDE.md` da raiz. Quando o texto diz "abaixo"/"acima" ou cita uma seção que não está aqui, procure-a pelo índice.

## Espécies: molde com vínculo — `module/species/species-rules.js` + `module/species/species.js`

Rework done (plan in `temp/plano-especies.md`; approved design: canvas "Rework de Espécies"), all seven phases: one engine and one application path for everything a Species gives, the editor, the Origem tab, sync, live passives, Linhagens, Heranças, Evolução, Anatomia com Funções e Implantes, default content and AI generation.

- **Live vs copied.** What belongs to the Species itself (Traços, elements, carry, and later passives/Escala/Deslocamento) is read live. What has per-character state — Body Parts (Vida, prosthesis, mods) and Racial Skills (level, XP) — is **copied with a stable key and an origin mark**: `flags.<system>.speciesGrant = {kind, id, key, hash?}` (`kind`: species/lineage/heritage). Anything without that mark (parts made by hand, bought Skills, Item-granted Skills) is never touched.
- **Pure engine** (`species-rules.js`, tested): `normalizeSpeciesEntry`/`normalizeSpeciesCatalog` (stable `key` on parts and Racial Skills — slug of the name when missing; `version`; empty `lineages`/`evolvesTo`/`passives`), `bumpSpeciesVersions` (version goes up only when parts/Skills change — that is what will drive "a Espécie mudou"), `resolveSpeciesTemplate` (Espécie → Linhagem → Heranças; `replaces.skills`/`replaces.parts` by key, Heranças `replaces.slots`; `removesTraits`; `unlockLevel` Skills held back in `locked`), `speciesDiff(current, template, {mode})` and `isSpeciesLocked`.
- **Diff rules:** parts match by key (legacy: slot + name), then by **slot** (the existing Item is *transformed*, so prostheses, mods and the Skills they grant survive); leftovers with prosthesis/mods are **detached** (become hand-made parts), empty ones are removed (`mode: "sync"` only removes with `removeMissing`). Current Vida follows the new max proportionally; a prosthesis keeps its own Vida. Racial Skills match by key (legacy: name); a removed one goes to `flags.<system>.speciesArchive` with level/XP and comes back restored if that origin returns.
- **One application path** (`species.js`): `previewSpeciesChange` → `applySpeciesPreview`, wrapped by `changeActorSpecies(actor, target, {interactive})`. The sheet, AI NPC/mount generation and the Agent's `propose_character` all go through it (the AI used to create Racial Skills with name/cost/description only, without the catalog's mechanics). A sheet with nothing granted yet (creation) applies directly; otherwise `apps/species-preview.js` shows the board-3 dialog (losses first, in red) and **nothing is written before "Aplicar"** — the species `<select>` has no `name` on purpose, so `submitOnChange` can't save it behind the dialog's back.
- **Player lock:** the first "Confirmar" of Attribute Points sets `speciesState.lockedAt`; from then on only the GM changes Espécie/Linhagem (`preUpdateActor` guard, select disabled). Sheets from before the rework with confirmed points count as locked. `speciesState.unlocked` (GM) lifts it until the next "Confirmar".
- **Editor (Phase 2, board 1)** — `apps/species-config.js` + `templates/apps/species-config.hbs`: grouped list with search on the left, the open Species on the right with six tabs (Geral · Corpo · Skills Raciais · Passivos · Linhagens · Evolução). It edits a **working copy** (`this.catalog`): every input carries `data-path` (relative to the open Species; `data-kind` int/optint/tags/listToggle) and writes on `change`; pickers write through the new `onChange` of `wirePickerField`. "Salvar" writes the whole catalog normalized and versioned. New rows are added by generic `addRow` (`data-list` path + `data-template`). Species/part/lineage keys are editable only until saved.
- **Origem on the sheet (Phase 2, board 4):** `originContext(actor)` (species.js) feeds the header and the new **Origem** tab (between Anatomia and Biografia). The header shows the Espécie/Linhagem selects only when there is no Species yet or for a player before the lock; otherwise a summary pill (lock icon, "Espécie mudou" badge). The tab shows each layer with what it gives (ao vivo × copiado), the history, and GM buttons (Trocar… → `pickSpeciesDialog` → preview, Linhagem Trocar…, Destravar, Ver e sincronizar…).
- **Sync (board 3 D):** `syncPreviewFor(actor)` = the sync-mode diff when non-empty; `openSpeciesSync(speciesId, {actors})` lists outdated sheets with a checkbox each (unlinked tokens unchecked by default) and keep/remove for what left the Species. Legacy data with no origin mark is **not** flagged (parts/Skills just get the mark on the next apply; mechanics are compared only when the mark carries a hash). Validar Mundo lists outdated sheets too. **The hash is versioned (1.78):** `skillDataHash` returns `"2-…"` over a key-sorted JSON of every level; before that it used `JSON.stringify(rest, topLevelKeys)`, which filtered nested objects away, so changing an effect's value never flagged the sheet. Saved marks keep the old hash — always compare with `skillHashMatches(saved, skill)` (new marks against the new hash, unprefixed ones against the old), never `===`, or changing the formula flags every sheet at once.
- **Live passives (Phase 3):** every origin layer (Espécie → Linhagem → Heranças) can carry `passives` (`attributeBonuses`, `statModifiers.{hp,energy}`, `resistances`, `conditionalModifiers`), `scale` and `movement: {base, percent}`. `actorOriginLayers(actor)` (`species/origin.js`, catalog → layers; kept apart from species.js so the DataModel never imports compendium/chat code) is computed once per preparation into the derived `system.originLayers`. Attribute bonuses enter `attr.total` **like a Title** (so Vida/Mana too — the owner's decision), `statModifiers` join `statModifierSources`, resistances count as a Title-kind source (best one wins), conditionals join `conditionalModifierSources`, `system.effectiveScale` = the sheet's Escala or the newest layer's, movement base = newest layer's, percents add. Traços and body elements now come from **all** layers (`originTraitIdsOf` — layers add, `removesTraits` take away; `actorElements` unions layer elements). Pure helpers in species-rules.js: `originLayers`, `originAttributeRows`, `originStatRows`, `originResistance`, `originConditionalSources`, `originScale`, `originMovement`. "De onde vem" lists them as "Espécie: X" / "Linhagem: Y" / "Herança: Z".
- **Linhagens and Heranças (Phase 4):** a Linhagem lives inside the Species entry (`lineages[]`: same block plus `replaces.skills`), one per sheet (`system.lineage`), chosen in the header at creation or by the GM in the Origem tab. Heranças are a separate catalog (`heritagesData`, `getActiveHeritages()`, defaults `NIHILITY.DEFAULT_HERITAGES`; editor `apps/heritages-config.js`, a subclass of the Species editor through the hooks `_readCatalog/_writeCatalog/_tabs/_blankEntry/_sheetsFor/_syncSheets/_extraContext/_wireCustomPicker`, tabs Geral · Corpo e Skills · Passivos · Regras). A sheet can have several (`system.heritages: [{id, acquiredAt, source}]`, newest wins in replacements). Heritage parts replace by **slot** (`replaces.slots`) because part keys differ per Species, and a template part can be `prosthetic: true` — the diff turns the existing part into the prosthesis (mods ride along) and turns a heritage prosthesis back into a natural part when the Herança leaves. `removesTraits` takes Traços away. Only the GM gives/takes a Herança (`changeActorHeritage(actor, id, {op, source})` — same preview, anúncio via Voz do Mundo with the Herança's `announce` text, `{nome}` replaced). `heritageConflicts` (pure) checks `allowedSpecies` (any/only/except) and `excludes` (both directions) and only **warns** ("Aplicar mesmo assim"). Validar Mundo checks sheet heritage/lineage ids and heritage catalog references.
- **Evolução (Phase 5, `FEATURES.speciesEvolution`, on by default):** a Species lists `evolvesTo: [{species, minLevel, hint, keepLineage, resistancesToImmunity}]` (editor tab Evolução, with the computed "Vem de"). The GM's "Evoluir…" in the Origem tab (`openEvolution`) shows the destinations as cards (`evolutionOptions`, pure — `minLevel` only warns), then the preview in diff mode **"evolution"**: the body changes like a normal change (parts without a pair leave, parts with implants become loose), but Racial Skills without a pair **stay** with level/XP (evolving adds, never erases what was learned). The Linhagem stays if the destination has it and `keepLineage`; Heranças always stay, with a warning when one stops fitting. With `resistancesToImmunity`, elemental Resistance Skills go to the max level and are renamed (`evolutionResistanceUpgrades`, pure; Geral never becomes Imunidade). Recorded in the history, announced by Voz do Mundo.
- **Anatomia com Funções e Implantes (Phase 6, `FEATURES.bodyPartInjury`, child of `anatomy`, **off by default**):** no organ simulation — the Part stays the unit, and what an organ *does* is a **Função** of the part (`system.functions` on `body_part`; catalog `bodyFunctionsData`, `getActiveBodyFunctions()`, defaults `NIHILITY.DEFAULT_BODY_FUNCTIONS`, editor `apps/body-functions-config.js`). Species part `tags` become functions through `legacyFunctionsFor(tags, slot)` (limb on arm → manipulacao, leg → locomocao, tail → equilibrio, wing → voo; every head sees and hears) — that is also the fallback for parts saved before the rework. Each Função has `count` (proportional / perPart = any loss / once = all lost) and an `effect` (condition, notify, movement, removeTrait, regen). `bodyFunctionState` (pure, `species/anatomy-rules.js`) gives the conditions, removed Traços, the movement factor and the crawl minimum; `deriveBodyState` (character-model, after `deriveMovement`) stores `system.bodyState` and applies `injuredMovement`: **Deslocamento × (working locomotion parts ÷ locomotion parts)**, a wounded part counts by its Vida when `woundedByHp`, and with no working locomotion part the character crawls `crawlMeters` (1 m) if a `crawlFunction` (manipulacao) part still works, else 0; a Species with no locomotion parts is unaffected. The designated GM keeps the condition Active Effects in sync (`syncBodyInjuryEffects`, flag `bodyInjury`, debounced from item/actor hooks), whispers when a `notify` (vital) part reaches 0, and heals `regen` parts at the start of the turn. **Implants** are Item Geral with `system.implant` (`kind` implant|prosthesis, `fitsSlots`, `location`, `functions`, `hp`; new "Implante" tab on the Item sheet, "Enquanto equipado" becomes "Enquanto instalado"): dropping one on a part row in the Anatomia tab calls `installImplant` (refuses a wrong slot — `implantFits` —, stores the Item in the mod's `sourceItem`, a prosthesis turns the part prosthetic with the implant's Vida and remembers `naturalHpMax`, grants the Item's Skill, takes 1 from the inventory); `uninstallImplant` reverses it and gives the Item back. While dragging, rows highlight green/red (`registerImplantDragTracking` remembers what is being dragged from any window). Turning the block off removes the body conditions and restores movement; implants keep working as plain modifications.
- **Part Vida in %, part states, Cura × Regeneração × Reparo (1.61.0, canvas board 5 second row):** a body part can store `hpPercent` (% of the character's **current** Vida máxima, buffs included — otherwise a big buff leaves every part at 0% with a full Vida bar) plus `integrity` (0–1, the saved proportion); `hp.max`/`hp.value`/`status` are then derived by `derivePartVitals` (character-model, after `deriveVitalStats`, before `deriveBodyState` — the prep order changed for this). `hpPercent: 0` is the old fixed Vida, so nothing saved changes meaning. `BodyPartDataModel._preUpdate` converts a `system.hp.value` write into `integrity` for % parts, so sheets and old writers keep working; new writers go through `partWrite` (anatomy.js). States: intact/damaged/destroyed (= **Inutilizada**, Vida 0) and **`lost`** (Perdida, a persisted flag; a prosthesis over a lost stump counts as the prosthesis). Pure and tested in `species/anatomy-rules.js`: `partHpPercentForSlot`, `partMaxFromPercent`, `resolvePartVitals`, `pickHitPart` (weighted by part size, lost parts excluded), `partDamage` (Perdida by overflow ≥ `partLossOverflow` % of the part — feature option of `bodyPartInjury`, default 50, 0 = never — or by the element effect `sever` reaching 0; a prosthesis never becomes lost), `partHealing` (same fraction of the heal on each part: `cura` wounded/disabled natural parts; `regeneracao` also regrows lost ones from 0% and, from a Unique/Ultimate Skill — `repairsProsthesis` flag stored on the periodic effect —, prostheses; `reparo` prostheses only; `blocked` = nothing). Damage: `damageApplyFlags` carries `bodyPartId` (from `promptTargetBodyPart`, "Mirar numa parte?", feature option `aimBodyPart`, single-target only) and `sever`; the chat **Aplicar** calls `damageBodyPart` with what reached Vida and stores `applied.bodyPart.before` so **Desfazer** restores it (`restoreBodyPart`). Area damage (applied by hand) and periodic ticks do not touch parts. Healing: effect entries have `healKind` (Skill editor + item sheet, only for a positive periodic hp entry; Condition catalog heal ticks too — `resolveConditionEffect` passes it); `tickPeriodicEffect` calls `healBodyParts`. **Impede regeneração**: a Condition catalog flag `blocksRegeneration` (default Condition `regen-blocked`); `isRegenerationBlocked(actor)` also honours an effect flag `regenBlocked`, which `applyRegenBlock` (damage-roll.js) uses when a saved catalog lacks the Condition. Element effect types `sever` (chance) and `blockRegen` (chance, `rounds` — 0 = until removed —, optional `conditionId`); default Fogo/Ácido block for 2 rounds. A blocked Regeneração tick does nothing at all (not even Vida). Parts do **not** regenerate by themselves (the owner's call): the old "regenerativa" Function and its `regen` effect kind were removed — regeneration is always a Skill/Condition heal tick with `healKind: "regeneracao"` (default Regeneração Amorfa/Carne Instável carry it); a saved Function catalog with `kind: "regen"` is simply ignored. Species parts carry `hpPercent` (defaults filled from `NIHILITY.PART_HP_PERCENT_BY_SLOT` after the object is built; saved catalogs stay fixed until the editor's **"% pelo slot"** + sync); the diff compares % rather than the derived number and carries `integrity`. Implants: `implant.hpMode` percent|fixed (old enabled implants migrate to fixed); installing a prosthesis stores `naturalHpPercent`/`naturalIntegrity` to restore on removal.
- **Healing as a pool, heal blocks with level, Antimagia, curses, regeneration and rest (1.63.0, canvas boards 6–8):**
  - *Pool healing* (`partHealing`, pure): what Vida actually rose is distributed among the parts this heal can fix — the focused part first (`promptHealFocus`, "Focar a cura numa parte?" on a single-target heal Skill, stored as `focusPartId` on the periodic effect), the rest proportional to what is missing. `partHealable` decides per kind (`descanso` only wounded natural parts; `cura` wounded/disabled, and lost ones when the heal level ≥ `cureRegrowLevel` (default 10); `regeneracao` also lost, prostheses from a Unique/Ultimate Skill; `reparo` prostheses). `healCap`: a part at 0% this heal can't fix blocks its % of Vida máx — that is what lets a later Regeneração find Vida to regrow a limb.
  - *Level on every effect:* `applyEffectsToActor` stores `sourceLevel` (Skill level; manual = 0; element on-hit = hit level via `mech.sourceLevel`) and `magic` (`skillIsMagic`, or the hit's magic) on every Active Effect it creates; reapplying with a higher level raises it.
  - *Heal blocks* replace "Impede regeneração": a Condition's `healBlock: {kinds: "regen"|"all", scope: "body"|"part"}` (legacy `blocksRegeneration`/flag `regenBlocked` = regen/body). `healBlockReduction` (core/magic-rules.js, pure): full block while heal level ≤ block + block/3, then linear down to 0 at `healBlockZeroMultiplier` × that limit (default 2), <1% = none. Part-scoped blocks store `healBlockPartId` (the part hit, from the Aplicar, or picked when the Skill is used). `healPlan(actor, {kind, level})` (anatomy.js) bundles body factor, per-part factor, regrow and cap — used by heal ticks, passive regeneration and rest. Rest, natural regen, Titles and Species are level 0.
  - *Antimagia* (`skills/dispel.js`, effect target `antimagic`, group "Magia"; it **ends** everything magical, never pauses — owner's decision 12/11; structures: see "Elementos, contato e Antimagia em Estruturas"): reach = Skill level × `antimagicReach` (default 1.5), or an explicit `reach` option for antimagic fields. Deletes magic Active Effects of level ≤ reach (curses included; suppression and body-injury effects stay), removes Escudo pools of magic Skills, switches off the target's magic Active Skills, and applies/refreshes the Condition flagged `suppressesMagic` (effect flag `suppression` = reach level). `core/suppression.js` (light, imported by the DataModel): while suppressed, magic Skills (non-racial by default, racial only if they spend energy — `skillIsMagic`), Item Geral marked `magic` and implants copied from them stop counting in attribute bonuses, stat modifiers, conditionals, Resistances and regeneration; magic Skills of level ≤ suppression can't be used; Skills used ON the suppressed target don't land when magic or a heal (`shielded` in applyEffectsToActor, single-target magic damage nulled in rollSkillDamage; area damage still hits). Curses/suppression can only be removed by the GM from the sheet.
  - *Curses* (`core/curse-rules.js` pure, `skills/curses.js`): a Condition with `curse: {enabled, effects[], costPercent, onUnpaid, worsenStep, worsenCap}` becomes ONE Active Effect (modifier effects as changes, tick effects run by `tickCurses`), level = the casting Skill's, `magic: true`. At the victim's turn start (after upkeep) the cost is always taken from their energy; when short: `hp` (missing from Vida), `sleep` (effect disabled until a full payment), `worsen` (+step% per unpaid round up to cap, never undone), `continue`. Effect entries can override per Skill (`curseOnUnpaid`). Default example: Maldição de Sangue.
  - *Regeneration per round* (`core/regen-rules.js` pure, `combat/regeneration.js`): `FEATURES.naturalRegen` (off by default, option `naturalEnergyRegenPercent`) + the `regen` block (`energyPercent`, `hpPercent`, `hpKind`) on Skill (scaled by level Power; Active only when on), Title, equipped Item Geral and origin passives (`passives.regen`), template `nihility.regenBlock`. Ticked at the start of the character's turn before upkeep (`tickRegeneration`); Vida per round heals per (kind, level) through `healPlan`. "De onde vem" lists the sources.
  - *Rest* (`openRestDialog`, the sheet's "Descansar…"): Curto (`restShortHpPercent`/`restShortEnergyPercent`, defaults 25/50) or Completo, with preview (`restOutcome`, pure): parts at 0% hold their % of Vida, the Vida gained is shared among wounded parts, body-wide "toda cura" blocks hold it entirely. Energy always returns.
- **Content and AI (Phase 7):** default Racial Skills that describe a rule now carry it (Couro Grosso/Escamas/Blindagem/Carapaça → physical Resistance, Sopro Dracônico → fire cone, Regeneração Amorfa/Carne Instável → Active periodic heal, Fúria Crescente → "Quando → Então", Presença de Dragão → fear in area from level 10…); Dragoide, Elfo and Androide have Linhagens; Slime → Slime Demoníaco and Ogro → Kijin are evolution chains (the evolved forms are `availableAtCreation: false`). Default species attribute bonuses stay at 0 (owner's decision). Saved catalogs don't get new default content by themselves — the editor's **"Trazer do padrão…"** adds missing entries and Linhagens/Evolução without overwriting edits. AI: `generateOriginFromAI(prompt, {kind})` (tasks "species"/"heritage" in the assistant, cards in Criar com IA) and the Agent's `propose_species`, both through `originGuide` + `sanitizeOriginEntry` (pure, `ai/ai-mechanics.js`); a result's `uuid` is `species:<id>`/`heritage:<id>`, which opens the editor on that entry.
- **Keys are structure:** the Species key and the part keys are read-only in the editor once saved; the editor preserves a Racial Skill's `key`/`unlockLevel` across the Skill editor, merges fields it doesn't show yet, and normalizes + versions on save. Migration `speciesStableKeys` writes keys into an already-saved catalog; sheets are not migrated (the diff recognises legacy data and marks it on first apply).
- **Fraquezas, Condições na origem e a tabela de fábrica (1.117.0).**
  - *Fraqueza* = valor **negativo** em `passives.resistances` de Espécie, Linhagem ou Herança (o editor aceita qualquer negativo e põe a etiqueta "resiste"/"fraqueza"; a Linhagem ganhou os blocos de Resistências e de Condições no cartão aberto). `originResistance` continua só olhando os ≥ 0; `originWeakness`/`originWeaknessRows` somam os negativos (pure, `species-rules.js`). **A conta:** resistência final do elemento = (Skill + melhor Título/origem, como sempre) **menos** a soma das fraquezas da origem, **sem piso** (dono, 24/11: −100% dobra, −300% quadruplica) — `netResistance(resist, weakness)`; **a Imunidade vale antes** (melhor fonte ≥ 100% → imune, a fraqueza não fura). A cadeia Geral → Físico → Cortante continua multiplicando as camadas e as fraquezas de cada camada da cadeia **somam** antes do `netResistance`. `resistanceSourceFor` ganhou `weakness`/`weaknessRows` (o `percent` continua ≥ 0); `resolveDamageParts` aceita Resistência negativa (multiplica o que sobrou por 1 + fraqueza **depois** das defesas; a Penetração não a reduz; **não conta como bloqueio**, então não rende XP de Resistência; Dano Absoluto a ignora). Rastro do Mestre: linha vermelha "Fraqueza a Fogo (Espécie: Slime) +20%" (`kind: "weak"`). O Simulador de encontro usa o mesmo `applyDamageReductions`. "Resistências viram Imunidade" na Evolução só olha Skills de Resistência: fraqueza da origem não mexe. Análise (degrau "Fraquezas") lista também as fraquezas da origem.
  - *"De onde vem" da Resistência:* `StatBreakdownApp` aceita `resist:<alvo>` (ⓘ ao lado de cada defesa na seção "Defesas da origem" da aba Origem): parcelas da Skill, do Título/origem e das fraquezas, com o total (`explainResistance`, pure, `combat/stat-explain.js`; as parcelas vêm de `resistanceBreakdown`, que usa a mesma `resistanceSourceFor` do dano).
  - *Condições na origem (1.117.0; Resistência refeita na 1.119.0):* `passives.conditions = [{conditionId, mode: "resist"|"immune", amount}]` (Imune = 100; normalizada por `normalizeOriginConditions`, que o `normalizeSpeciesEntry` aplica também às Linhagens). Regra pura de origem: `originConditionRule(layers, id)` (qualquer Imune ou Resistência ≥ 100 = imune; senão a **melhor** %). **Resistência não é imunidade (dono, 24/11): o d100 da 1.117.0 foi trocado por exposição acumulada.** Regras puras em `combat/condition-resist-rules.js` (`test/condition-resist.test.mjs`): cada **exposição** soma `1 − X` (X = Resistência, 0–1) num contador por Ator e por Condição (flag `conditionExposure.<id>` = `{count, hits, combatId, round, time}`); a Condição **pega quando o contador chega a 1** (`exposureNeeded`: 30% → 2ª exposição, 75% → 4ª, 90% → 10ª; sem resistência = 1ª, como sempre) e o contador é apagado ao pegar. Ao pegar, a **força** (`amount`, `hitDamage`: dano ou cura por rodada e qualquer número do efeito) e a **duração** (`durationRounds`, mínimo 1 rodada se havia prazo) saem × (1 − X), arredondadas (um número que não era zero nunca vira zero: `scaleEntryByResistance`); Condição sem número (Medo, Cegueira) só demora. **Zera** depois de 3 rodadas sem exposição (opção `conditionResistResetRounds` do bloco Ambiente, afinável em Módulos do Sistema; `exposureExpired`) e na troca de combate; **fora de combate** decidimos pelo relógio real (6 s por "rodada", ou seja 18 s com o padrão), porque o relógio do mundo pode nem andar. Quem **já tem** a Condição é renovação: não conta exposição (só a força e a duração saem reduzidas, sem texto de chat). **Ponto único:** `checkOriginCondition(actor, conditionId, {pierce, dedupe})` em `combat/condition-guard.js` devolve `{lands, kind: none|immune|resist|pierced, factor, hits, needed}`; chamado por `applyEffectsToActor` (todo efeito com `conditionId`: Skill, elemento por chance, "ao acertar", reação, superfície, duelo, Armadilha com efeito; depois do padrão da Condição reduz a entrada e posta a linha "pegou com força e duração −30%"), `tickContagion` (cada contágio que pegaria é uma exposição; `infect(…, originResist)` reduz) e `applyCondition(..., {guard: true})` (Armadilha que só põe a marca). **O ambiente da cena/Região agora também passa pela resistência** (antes não): `syncNow` (environment.js) conta **uma exposição por rodada** (`dedupe: true`: `sameExposureWindow`; entrar e andar na mesma rodada não conta de novo; fora de combate, uma a cada 6 s de relógio real) e manda `originResist` ao `applyCondition`, que reduz a força do efeito; o início do turno do Token já sincronizava, então cada rodada dentro da área é uma exposição. A imunidade é silenciosa no ambiente (sem aviso a cada rodada). **Não consultam, de propósito:** Condição marcada à mão no HUD (`applyManualCondition(..., {force: true})`; o Mestre vê o aviso de `warnManualOverride` e a Condição aplica mesmo assim) e Âncora/Domínio/Antimagia (nunca resistidos; o Validar Mundo avisa quem tem Imunidade a elas: `isSystemHoldingCondition`). **"Fura imunidade e resistência a Condição" (1.119.0):** campo `piercesCondition` em **cada entrada de efeito** (`effectEntrySchema`; checkbox no cartão de efeito da ficha, no editor rápido e na frase "Sangramento, fura imunidade") e `onHit.pierce` no "ao acertar" (`normalizeOnHit`, `onHitPicks`, `onHitConditionsFor` → `applyTriggeredConditions` põe `piercesCondition` na entrada): a Condição pega mesmo em quem é Imune e sem a demora e a redução (`checkOriginCondition(..., {pierce: true})`). Pega na próxima." com a flag `conditionResist` (sussurrada ao Mestre se o Token está escondido); todo cliente sobe o texto ao ver a linha (`registerConditionFloats`, segue a opção `floatingNumbers`); ao pegar, "Anão pegou Veneno com força e duração −30%: 7 por rodada, 2 rodadas (era 10 por 3).". **Limites conhecidos:** a Maldição (curse) e as Condições do catálogo sem efeito numérico só sentem a demora; o contador é por Ator (Token não vinculado tem o próprio); em combates seguidos o contador zera na troca de combate; o ambiente fora de combate depende de alguém mover/sincronizar o Token para contar a exposição.

  - *Migração `originDefenses`* (`core/migrations.js` → `planOriginDefenseMigration`, pure, `species/origin-defense-migration-rules.js`): no catálogo **salvo** de Espécies e de Heranças, cada campo (Resistências, Condições e, no Lobo Tempestade, Elementos do corpo) recebe o valor novo **só onde ainda é igual ao de fábrica anterior** (`OLD_FACTORY_DEFENSES`); o que o Mestre editou fica e entra num **único aviso sussurrado aos Mestres** no fim. Idempotente. Ao mudar a fábrica de novo, acrescente a tabela antiga ao `old` em vez de reaproveitar a chave.
