# Estruturas, Natureza (magia/tecnologia), PEM e campos

> Parte do guia do projeto. O índice e as regras que valem em todo lugar estão no `CLAUDE.md` da raiz. Quando o texto diz "abaixo"/"acima" ou cita uma seção que não está aqui, procure-a pelo índice.

## Estruturas — `module/structures/structures.js` + `module/structures/structure-geometry.js`

Skills whose **Mecânica ao Usar** is `effectType: "structure"` (+ `structureId`, from the `structuresData` catalog edited in `apps/structures-config.js`; block `FEATURES.structures`) raise walls and blocks on the map. A structure becomes **real Foundry Walls** (so it blocks movement, and sight if configured — and the per-round movement limit already stops at walls), created by the designated GM through the relay (`createStructure`; the definition is read from the catalog on the GM side, never trusted from the payload). Walls are invisible to players, so what shows on the map is drawn by **every client** from the scene registry (`module/structures/structure-render.js`, a PIXI container in `canvas.interface`, redrawn on `canvasReady` and whenever the scene flag changes): a thick band along the walls in the catalog color (or the catalog image as texture), fill on closed shapes, the name and a Vida bar (numbers only for the GM; a mana barrier's bar is the caster's Mana, and it pulses). It is **not** a Drawing document on purpose: in 1.37 a Drawing was created between the walls and the registry, its creation failed in a real world, and the error left orphan walls that nothing could remove (not even switching the Skill off). Keep the order walls → registry → chat card, and keep the visual document-free. The registry stores `segments`/`shape`/`color`/`img` for the renderer; `removeOrphanStructureWalls` (designated GM, on `canvasReady`) deletes walls flagged `structureInstance` whose instance is not in the registry. **Falling:** `removeStructureInstance(scene, id, reason)` removes the instance and, in the same scene update, records `structureFalls.<id> = {reason, label, time}` (`destroyed`, `manaDepleted`, `expired`, `dismissed`, `casterDown`; entries older than 10 min are pruned in that same write). Walls and lights go immediately — game state never waits for a visual — while every client's renderer notices the instance disappeared since its last draw (`lastInstances`) and plays a short fall animation from the last shape it knew (shatter / flicker / dissolve by reason, `playFall`); the chat card reads the same record to say how it fell. **Skill animation vs. structures:** `interceptingStructure` also returns the impact `point`; single-target damage Skills play their Sequencer animation inside `rollSkillDamage` (not in `useSkillEffect`), stopping at the impact point and continuing impact → target only when damage passed through (`playSkillAnimation(…, {impact, passesThrough})`).

**Estruturas bloqueiam ataques.** Walls only stop movement and sight; damage never looked at them, so a shot at someone inside a barrier went straight to the token. Every damage path now traces a line from the attacker's token center (or the area's origin, carried as `targetActors.origin` from `pickAreaTargets`) to the target's token center; the first Estrutura crossed (`firstStructureOnPath`/`segmentCrossing`, pure, tested) takes the hit first, up to its capacity — its Vida, or the caster's Mana for a mana barrier — and only the rest (`splitStructureHit`) goes on to the target's defenses (`throughStructures` in combat/damage-roll.js, used by `rollSkillDamage`, `fireStarshipWeapon` and `rollSkillDamageArea`). Rules chosen: it happens **before** the target's reductions, and even Dano Absoluto is stopped (a wall is cover, not a resistance); a structure never blocks attacks of **the Actor who raised it** (you shoot out of your own barrier); in an area attack the structure is hit **once** and everyone behind it gets the same passing fraction. The damage to the structure is written immediately by the GM (`damageStructure` relay action), like a ship's cascade — it is environment bookkeeping; the target's own damage still waits for Aplicar. When one Actor has several Tokens, the selected Token is the attacker and the targeted Token is the target (`tokenOnCanvas`).

**Luz (Estrutura e Escudo pessoal) — `module/combat/lights.js`.** One editor, `openLightConfigDialog`, used in the Estruturas catalog card, in a Skill Effect whose target is "Escudo" (item sheet card and the Skill editor modal), stored as `normalizeLightConfig` (config.js, pure, tested: `null` = no light; radius 0 = automatic, the caller decides). It has a **live preview** on the map, only on the editing client: an *unsaved* AmbientLight document drawn in `canvas.lighting.preview` (the same trick Foundry uses while you drag a new light), on the selected Token or the view center, updated on every field change, removed when the window closes — nothing is saved until Aplicar. Foundry's own animations (Campo de Energia, Domo Hexagonal, Grade de Força…) are what make an energy shield. A **Estrutura** with light gets real AmbientLight documents created with its walls (one in the center of a closed shape; several spread along a line with `pointsAlongPolyline`), its walls stop blocking light (they would cut their own glow in half), the ids go into the registry (`lightIds`), and they are **deleted** with the walls — never hidden/turned off; orphan cleanup covers lights too. A light failing to create must not stop the registry (the 1.37 lesson). A **personal Escudo** lights the *Token* (it follows the character): `setShieldLight` (relay — the caster rarely owns the target's Token) saves the Token's original light in the `shieldLight` flag once and applies the new one; when the Escudo value reaches 0 (`updateActor`, designated GM) or the Active Skill that gave it is switched off (`removeUpkeepLinkedEffects` → `clearShieldLights`), the light is **deleted**: the Token gets its original light back and the flag is removed. The owner asked for exactly that — "apagada, não desligada". Shapes: `line` (two clicks), `free` (click point by point, Enter ends, Backspace undoes, ends by itself when the length runs out), `circle` (radius), `rect` (side); `structureSegments`/`capPolyline` (pure, tested) cut the shape to the catalog size, exactly like the movement ruler stops at the limit. Instances live in the scene flag `structures`; a chat card (`renderStructureControls`, GM-only buttons) shows HP and lets the GM apply damage or knock it down. Rules decided with the owner: HP > 0 falls at 0; **HP 0 = mana barrier** (damage comes out of the caster's Mana); duration > 0 ticks per round (`advanceStructures` in the `updateCombat` hook); duration 0 + Active Skill lasts until the Skill is switched off (`removeUpkeepLinkedEffects` → `removeStructuresFor`); duration 0 otherwise lasts until the GM removes it or the caster's HP or Mana reaches 0 (`collapseStructuresOfCaster`, `updateActor` hook), and a mana barrier always falls when the caster's Mana hits 0. With no map, the Skill only posts the card.

Estrutura is a **mechanic, not a Tipo de Alvo**. In 1.37 it was `targetType: "structure"`, which let the editors offer nonsense combinations ("Dano" + Estrutura, and the target field had to be shown even for Descritiva); the shape and size come from the catalog, so there is no target to choose. The Tipo de Alvo list (`SKILL_TARGET_TYPES`) no longer has it, and both editors hide the whole Alvo section for Descritiva and Estrutura. The schema still *accepts* `targetType: "structure"` so old data validates: `migrateData` on Skill, Item Geral, Módulo and Parte do Corpo converts it (`migrateStructureTarget`, in the Skill, its Sub-Skills and every Habilidade Concedida template), and runtime code asks `isStructureMechanic(mech)` (both forms), never `targetType === "structure"`.

**Material, Vida-da-Mana, nível, aliados, contato e regeneração (owner's decisions, 11/11; plan in `temp/revisao-estruturas.md`).** Catalog fields `material` (`physical`/`ethereal`) and `manaPowered` ("A Vida é a Mana de quem ergueu"); fallbacks for saved catalogs/instances without them are pure (`structureMaterial`: `hp === 0` → ethereal; `structureManaPowered`: `hp === 0 && blocksAttacks !== false`, structure-geometry.js). Both are copied onto the instance (`material`, `manaBarrier` ← `manaPowered`), plus `level` (the Skill/Sub-Skill level, read GM-side from the source actor's sheet in `createStructureAsGm`, never from the payload — Antimagia will compare it) and `disposition` (the caster's Token disposition); `instanceInfo` exposes `material`. **Dano Absoluto** (`absolute` flag threaded `rollSkillDamage`/`rollSkillDamageArea`/`fireStarshipWeapon` → `throughStructures`/`hitStructure`) against an **ethereal** structure removes it (`reason: "broken"`, relay `damageStructure` with `broken: true`) and the whole damage passes; against a **physical** one it is stopped as any hit. **Who passes** (`structureLetsPass`, pure): a physical structure blocks everyone, the caster included; an ethereal one lets the caster and allies (same Token disposition as the stored one) through; an old instance without `disposition` keeps "only the caster passes" for ethereal ones. **Unsupported:** when the Active Skill that sustained a structure stops (`removeStructuresForAsGm`: switched off, Mana 0, Antimagia, Suppression) a physical, non-mana-powered one still tied to it (`untilDeactivated`) stays standing with Vida 1 and `untilDeactivated: false` (lasts until destroyed or removed by the GM); ethereal or mana-powered ones are removed (`dismissed`); structures already untied are left alone. `collapseStructuresOfCaster`: a physical untimed one survives its caster at Vida/Mana 0; ethereal ones fall as before; mana-powered ones always fall when the Mana hits 0. **Contact** now also burns when the movement ENDS touching a wall (`touchesSegments`/`distanceToSegments`, pure: centre-to-wall distance ≤ half the Token's width + 5% of a square) and at the start of the turn of a combatant still touching or inside a closed structure (`tickStructureContact`, called from `updateCombat` next to the radiation tick); at most one burn per structure per movement. **Regeneration of a maintained structure** (Skill fields `structureRegen` Y Vida/round and `structureRegenCost` X energy, in the shared mechanic schema and every copy list next to `variableMana`; only meaningful in an Active Skill, lint `structureRegenNotActive`): `regenerateSustainedStructures` (structures.js), called in `upkeep.js` right after `regenerateSustainedShields`, for the holder's instances still tied to the Skill, not mana-powered and hurt: pays `effectiveSkillCost(X, level) × investRatio`, heals `Y × skillLevelBonuses(level).power` up to `hpMax` (`structureRegenStep`, pure, tested); not enough energy → nothing, the structure stays; full Vida → no cost; no XP; one chat line.


**Natureza das coisas e PEM (backlog C1, 12/11).** Toda coisa pode ser **Mágica**, **Tecnológica**, as duas (tecnomágica) ou nenhuma (analógica, imune aos dois). Skill/Sub-Skill/Habilidade Concedida: `isMagic`/`isTech` (nullable booleans in `usageFields()`; `null` = not chosen, so the old `magicTag` + auto rule still decides magic, and tech is only Nave/Veículo by nature — nothing saved changed meaning; saving the sheet writes the resolved value). Item Geral: `magic` + `tech` (null = default: implant, prosthesis and `isPadDevice` are tech, `itemIsTech`); mods copy `tech`; Estruturas catalog: `magic`, `tech`, `interferenceLevel`. UI is two checkboxes "Mágico"/"Tecnológico" (Skill sheet "Avançado ▸", quick editor, Item Geral, Estruturas card); Condition card has "Selo de interferência (nível)" and "Suprime tecnologia". Pure rules: `isMagicUse`/`isTechUse`/`useNature`/`technomagicFactor` (rules.js), `skillIsTech`/`itemIsTech`/`itemNature`/`protectedFromKind`/`dispelledEffects(…, kind)` (magic-rules.js). **Tecnomágico counts as magic** for mana density and Antimagia, and as tech for the PEM; a tech-only use ignores mana density. A tecnomágico use multiplies its power (`investPower` in `useSkillEffect`, `suggestSkillCost`'s `technomagicFactor`, the Encounter simulator's `rawAverage`) by the world setting `technomagicPower` (Regras da Mesa, default 2, min 1). A Habilidade Concedida inherits the source's nature when the template left the box `null` (`buildGrantedSkillData(…, sourceNature)`; a Módulo grants tech Skills).

**PEM = Antimagia espelhada, uma implementação com `kind`.** `FEATURES.interference` (off by default; on in Sci-Fi/Misto presets) hides the `emp` effect target (group "Tecnologia") and the tech fields without deleting data. `skills/dispel.js` `applyDispel(target, kind)` (`applyAntimagic`/`applyEmp`): cuts effects flagged `tech` (effects now store `tech` beside `magic`, `sourceTech` in `applyEffectsToActor`), removes Escudo pools of tech Skills, switches off tech Active Skills, applies "Suprimido (Tecnologia)" (effect flag `techSuppression`, `suppressesTech` Condition; `core/suppression.js` `suppressionState(actor)` = `{magic, tech}`, `sourceSuppressed` takes that state) — tech Items, implants, prostheses, the PAD device (`hasPadDevice`) and tech Skills stop counting; a suppressed prosthesis is a disabled part (`partDisabled`, anatomy-rules.js) so locomotion/movement drop through the Anatomia rules. Reach = level × `antimagicReach`, same as Antimagia; XP por uso is the same `summary.xp`. **Fields/seals:** Structures `interferenceLevel` (default "Campo de Interferência"), Conditions `interferenceLevel` ("Selo de Interferência"); `structures.js` `FIELD_KINDS` generalises every field function with `kind` (`antimagicBetween`, `antimagicLevelAt`, `antimagicFieldLevelAtToken`, `enforceAntimagicOnStructures`, `structureEndedAtOnce`); `combat/antimagic.js` `chargeAntimagic` runs the magic and the tech kind (tecnomágico pays both), `endMagicInsideFields` both kinds (field suppression flag `interferenceField`), upkeep switches off tech Active Skills inside a tech field. Fields never touch fields of either kind; a tech Structure only ends under a tech field. Saved catalogs get the Conditions through migration `techConditions` (the Estruturas catalog does not — add "Campo de Interferência" in the editor). **Armas tecnológicas** (Item Geral `tech`) and Nave weapons: from **outside** into an interference field the shot spends its round/ammo and vanishes ("anulado pela interferência"; `mech.weaponShot` → `chargeKind` nulls, no energy); from **inside** the field (or under the Seal) nothing happens and nothing is spent (`fieldGate` before the magazine/ammo/cooldown). Analog weapons are unaffected. Tecnomágico is magic against Antimagia (Manipulação applies) and tech against Interferência (always vanishes). **PEM vs Nave/Veículo** (`applyEmpToShip`, starship-power.js): drops the `1 + ⌊reach÷5⌋` online Módulos that draw the most power (`moduleDisable`, Endurecimento can still hold) and drains `5% × reach` (max 75%) of the Bateria (`energyDrain`), for the effect's rounds (0 → 2). **Corpo mecânico** (Traço `mechanical`): PEM (Skill or field) also makes the actor Inconsciente with no counter (`applyEmpShutdown`, effect flag `empShutdown`), released with the tech Suppression (`deleteActiveEffect` hook / `releaseFieldSuppression`); an Inconsciente from Vida 0 is never touched. Racial Skills and origin passives are not suppressed by PEM. **Camada oposta protege** (`protectedFromKind`): an Escudo pool of the opposite-only nature (magic-only vs PEM, tech-only vs Antimagia; tecnomágico protects nothing) or a non-field Structure of the opposite kind on the line / enclosing the target (`protectingStructureKinds`, `enclosingStructureKinds`) stops the whole ending effect for that target, also for fields. Tests: `test/nature.test.mjs`.

**Elementos, contato e Antimagia em Estruturas.** Catalog entries carry `blocksAttacks` (false = never intercepts attacks: fields, fire walls), `elements`, `magic`, `antimagicLevel`, `contactDamage`; they are **copied onto the instance at creation** (`instanceInfo(instance)` reads them, falling back to the catalog for older instances). `hitStructure(block, amount, elementIds)` multiplies what the structure takes by `hitAffinityFactor` against the structure's elements (the element affinity table, see "Vantagem entre elementos") and converts what passes back to the hit's scale. Contact damage: `registerStructureContact` (`moveToken`, designated GM) checks the moved path (origin + `passed.waypoints`) against each structure (`segmentsCross`/`pointInSegments`, pure) and calls `applyStructureContactAsGm` (structures/structure-contact.js, dynamic import to avoid the cycle), which rolls, runs `applyDamageReductions` with the structure's elements and posts a normal damage card. **Antimagia:** the "Mágica" mark is `magicTag` (`auto`/`magic`/`mundane`, in `usageFields()` and in every mechanic copy list); `isMagicUse(mech, actor)` (pure) — auto = costs the character's energy or has Dano Mágico, ships never. Sources: structures with `antimagicLevel` and Conditions with `antimagicLevel` (`actorAntimagicLevel`; default catalog has "Selo Antimagia"). `antimagicLevelBetween` = seal + fields the attack line crosses / the attacker or target stands in; `chargeAntimagic` (combat/antimagic.js) decides by the **three-case field rule** below (it no longer charges `antimagicSurcharge`; that function and the `antimagicBase`/`antimagicGrowth` settings are now unused by the field flow). **A field catches everything magical (12/11), not only damage:** besides `rollSkillDamage`/`rollSkillDamageArea`, it is called by `applySkillEffects`/`applySkillEffectsArea` (buff/debuff/heal/Escudo, a nulled target gets "anulado pela antimagia"), and by magic Structure and Zone creation in `runSkillEffect`, where the "targets" are placement points (`placementTargets`; `antimagicBetween` accepts `{point}` targets) plus the line from the caster. **What is inside a field ends:** `endMagicInsideFields(tokenDoc)` (antimagic.js, designated GM) runs for a Personagem standing in a closed field at the start of its turn (`updateCombat`, not the dead) and when a Token ends a movement (`registerFieldAntimagic`, `moveToken`, works out of combat): `applyAntimagic(actor, {reach: field level, field: sceneId})` — `reach` is the field's `antimagicLevel` directly (no Skill ×1.5); the Suppression it applies is a separate effect flagged `antimagicField`, with **no duration**, kept while the Token is inside (`fieldSuppressionPlan`, pure) and removed by `releaseFieldSuppression` on the next trigger that finds it outside every closed field, or when the field structure is removed (`removeStructureInstance` re-checks the scene's tokens); a Skill's Suppression is never touched; a card and the field Skill's flat XP only when `summary.changed` > 0. **Structures** (`antimagicStructureOutcome`, pure, tested: level ≤ reach and (magic or sustained by a Skill or mana-powered) → ethereal/mana-powered `remove` (reason `antimagic`), physical sustained by a Skill `unsupport` (Vida 1, `untilDeactivated: false`, via `unsupportStructure`, also used when a sustaining Skill stops), physical magic raised once (no Skill holding it) `remove` (owner, 12/11), else `keep` — plain physical matter that is neither magic nor sustained is untouched): `enforceAntimagicOnStructures(scene, {charge})` applies it to every non-field structure touching a field, at each round change (`advanceStructures`) **and when a structure is created** (`createStructureAsGm`, `charge: false`, so it works out of combat); above the field's level a magic one stays and its caster keeps paying the per-round surcharge (or it dissolves without Mana). **Field geometry, three cases (Lote B, 12/11 — same for the Antimagia field/Seal and, mirrored, the Interferência one):** (1) a use **from outside** that crosses a field or whose target/placement is inside one is **paid normally** (Mana; a weapon spends its round/ammo; the Recarga starts — `postAntimagicNulled` now returns `true` so a consumable is spent too) and then **vanishes** if the field wins, or passes paying extra; (2) a use **from inside a field or under a Seal** of that kind **does nothing and spends nothing** — `fieldGate(actor, mech, {baseCost})` (antimagic.js) is called in `runSkillEffect` right after the Suppression checks (before Mana prompts, Preparo, overdraw, Active toggles-on), in `useWeaponAttack` and in `fireStarshipWeapon`, and refuses with a pt-BR warning ("Dentro do Campo de Antimagia, a ação de X não sai." / "Sob o Selo …"); the mechanic copy then carries `fieldGated: true` and `paidCost` (the Mana actually paid, investment included; prepaid releases use cost × ratio) so `chargeKind` does not charge an inside user twice (an un-gated inside use, e.g. a consumable, is treated as inside inside `chargeKind`). The old pre-payment refusals/nulls (`structureEndedAtOnce` in `runSkillEffect`, `fieldEndsEffectAt`) are gone: placing a Structure or Zone from outside into a field is paid, then the use vanishes or passes by the same rule, and a Structure that passes is then subject to the existing ethereal/physical rules. **Manipulação de [energia]** decides how strong the field is against the user: `fieldUseOutcome({inside, kind, manipulationLevel, fieldLevel, baseCost, zeroMultiplier})` (magic-rules.js, pure, tested) → `{action: "blocked"|"vanish"|"extra"|"pass", extra, factor, resisted}`: without the Skill, or for tech (no counter-Skill exists), f = 1 (outside → `vanish`, inside → `blocked`); with it f = `healBlockReduction(manipulation, field, zeroMultiplier)` — the same curve and the **same `healBlockZeroMultiplier` setting** as heal blocks (`getHealingRules().zeroMultiplier`); 0 < f < 1 → `extra` = `ceil(baseCost × f)` energy (no energy: outside → vanishes, inside → refused); f = 0 → `pass` free. A Seal counts as a field of its level. **Active magic Skills inside a field** (`tickActorUpkeepSkills`): per round the extra by the same curve on top of the upkeep, or switched off (f = 1); tech Active Skills always switch off. **Magic Structures touching a field** (`enforceFieldsOnStructures`): the caster's Manipulação decides per round — `blocked` or no energy for the extra → the structure is removed; else the extra is `ceil(per-round upkeep of the sustaining Skill × f)` (0 for a loose Structure). A Preparo is a use: starting one inside a field is refused; the extra is charged up front with the first payment. The Manipulação Skill earns the flat XP (`creditManipulation`) each time it lets something through (f < 1). Effects already inside a field still end by `endMagicInsideFields` (unchanged). Known limit: the inside check uses the user's own field/Seal level, not a higher field on the path to the target. Fields never affect fields. **The Antimagia Skill used as Emissão** also hits structures the area touches (`areaTouchesStructure`, pure; `pickAreaTargets` now sets `.area` on the returned array): the user asks the GM through the `antimagicStructures` relay action (`dispelStructuresInArea` → `antimagicStructuresAsGm`), which requires owning the Skill, recomputes the reach from the Skill's level on the sheet and checks the area fits the Skill's shape/size; chat lines "X: desfeita" / "X: sem sustento (Vida 1)" and flat XP per structure affected. **Pre-made Skills (`system.coreSkill`):** "Manipulação de [energia]" (`manaManipulation`) and "Detecção de [energia]" (`manaDetection`; descriptive for now, mechanics to be designed) are system Skills; data in `core/config/defaults/core-skills.js` (`MEU_SISTEMA.CORE_SKILL_DEFS`, `{E}` = the world's energy label), seeded into the world Skills compendium by `ensureCoreSkills` (called at the end of `ensureSystemCompendiums`, matched by `coreSkill`, never by name; created `magicTag: "mundane"`). `coreSkill` is a schema field on Skill and deliberately **not** in any mechanic copy list (fusion/evolution don't copy it). `actorManipulation(actor)` (config/actors.js) = highest level among the actor's Skills with that key (Item-granted count); `manipulationLevelOf` is the pure part. The Skill sheet shows a "Skill de base" chip; the Skill sentence says "resiste à Antimagia"; the Glossário has the "Manipulação" entry and Regras desta mesa a "Campo de Antimagia" paragraph.

**Escudo adaptativo** (Módulo de Escudo `adaptive`/`adaptStep`/`adaptCap`): the learned values live in the installed module's flag `shieldAdaptation` as `{"element@frequency": percent}` (`shieldAdaptationKey`/`adaptShield`/`shieldAdaptationFor`, pure). The attacker's frequency is the `weaponFrequency` Actor flag (set by the "Modular frequência" ship action, `modulateWeaponFrequency`). `resolveShipCascade` takes `shield.adaptation` (0–0.95, `shieldAdaptationAgainst` = best online adaptive shield) and cuts the whole hit while the shield is up; `applyStarshipDamageCascade` then either teaches the hit (`adaptShieldsToHit`) or, if the shield fell, clears everything (`clearShieldAdaptation`); a shield module going offline clears its own flag (`registerShieldAdaptationReset`, designated GM). `averageElementContext` now also returns `elementIds`.
